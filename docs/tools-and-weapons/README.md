# The Reliquary: Special Tools, Mythic Weapons & Regalia

> *"Mortals forge iron with fire and hammer. The gods forged their arms from the marrow of monsters, the curdled salt of the first sea, the feathers of zephyrs, and the embers of falling stars. To wield one is not merely to carry a weapon; it is to bear the weight of the myth that birthed it."*

---

## 1. The Design Philosophy of Relics

In *The World*, high-tier armaments and utility implements are not generic randomized stat sticks (+5 Longsword of Frost). Every single relic:
1. **Has Authentic Mythological Provenance:** Derived from canonical world texts (Homeric hymns, the Eddas, the Ramayana, the Kojiki, the Book of the Dead, West African Itans, Celtic cycle).
2. **Has Distinctive Mechanical Identity:** Relics do things generic weapons cannot do: change physical dimensions (*Ruyi Jingu Bang*), dredge sunken islands from the sea floor (*Manaiakalani*), fold into a pocket pouch (*Skíðblaðnir*), or blossom into 30 barbs inside an opponent (*Gáe Bulg*).
3. **Has Visual & Sonic Weight:** Each relic has custom 3D models, signature particle shaders, distinct audio signatures, and unique animation sets.

---

## 2. Core Game Systems: Attunement & Divine Burden

Carrying a weapon forged for a deity comes with cosmological consequences:

### A. Attunement & The Worthiness Check
A character cannot simply loot a divine relic and immediately swing it with maximum proficiency.
- **The Call of the Relic:** Each relic demands specific character alignments, feats, or mythological trials.
  - *Mjölnir* requires high physical fortitude and the *Járngreipr* (Iron Gauntlets) or else lightning will recoil into the wielder's nervous system.
  - *Ruyi Jingu Bang* requires mastery of martial discipline or else it weighs 13,500 catty and cannot be lifted from the floor.
  - *Gáe Bulg* can only be launched while standing barefoot between the surf of crashing waves.

### B. Divine Burden (The Cost of Power)
Divine items channel primordial energy that mortal bodies were never engineered to sustain:
- **Stamina Drain:** Using a relic's primary god-strike burns stamina chunks and imposes short cooldown windows.
- **Aggro Aura (Divine Resonance):** Activating a legendary relic emits a sonic or magical beacon that alerts nearby roaming world bosses (such as sea serpents, valkyries, or dragon kings) across several nautical leagues.
- **No Durability Loss, but Soul Exhaustion:** Divine relics never break or lose durability. Instead, their powers become dormant if the player's spirit energy is exhausted, reverting temporarily to high-grade blunt metal until recharged at sacred shrines (e.g. the Well of Segais, the Forge of Hephaestus, or the Springs of Enki).
