# Island Spec: Xibalba & Diyu (The Underworld Isles)

> *"Then the Hero Twins, Hunahpu and Xbalanque, descended the steep road to Xibalba, the Place of Fear. They crossed the River of Churning Waters, the River of Blood, and the River of Pus, passing between the great canyon walls into the subterranean courts of the Twelve Death Lords."*  
> — *Popol Vuh (The Book of the Dawn of Life, Mayan K'iche')*

---

## 1. Mythological Provenance

- **Traditions:** Mayan Mythology (*Popol Vuh*) & Chinese Taoist/Buddhist Afterlife (*Diyu / The Ten Courts of Yanluo Wang*).
- **Historical Archetype:** The subterranean water-realm of judgment, trials, and rebirth:
  1. **Xibalba (Xibalbá):** The underworld of the Maya Lords of Death (Hun-Came and Vucub-Came), entered through sacred underwater cave portals (*Cenotes*) and subterranean river networks.
  2. **Diyu (地獄):** The purgatorial realm of ten courts bordered by the Yellow Springs (*Huangquan*), crossed via the Bridge of Forgetfulness (*Naihe Bridge*) guarded by Meng Po.
- **Cosmological Foundation:** In *The World*, this island serves as the **Subterranean Waterway**: an abyssal cenote island whose subterranean caverns connect to death, respawn trials, and lost underworld treasures.

### Key Sub-Zones

1. **The Great Sinkhole Cenote (The Gateway of Bones):** A breathtaking circular karst sinkhole dropping 200 meters into subterranean emerald waters, framed by trailing jungle lianas, ancient stepped Mayan pyramids, and stone jaguar altars.
2. **The Six Houses of Trial:** Legendary chambers from the *Popol Vuh* designed by the death lords to test mortal souls:
   - *Dark House (Quequma-ha):* Utter darkness where no torch can stay lit; players must navigate by sound and tactile echolocation.
   - *Razor House (Chayim-ha):* Blades fly through the air on automated wind currents.
   - *Cold House (Xuxulim-ha):* Bitter freezing subterranean blizzards that shatter brittle gear.
   - *Jaguar House (Balami-ha):* Filled with hungry spirit jaguars that must be placated or fought.
   - *Fire House (Ygquinim-ha):* Roaring sulfur furnaces and magma cataracts.
   - *Bat House (Zotzi-ha):* The lair of Camazotz, the death bat deity whose razor-wings decapitate careless travelers.
3. **The Naihe Bridge & Pavilion of Meng Po:** A narrow arched bridge spanning the subterranean Yellow Springs where spirits cross to be judged. Meng Po's pavilion offers the *Soup of Oblivion* (used for character stat respecs).
4. **The Pok-ta-Pok Underworld Ballcourt:** A stone ballcourt with high carved rings where players can challenge death champions to ritual ballgames to earn instant revival tokens for fallen companions.

---

## 3. Zonal Physics & Environmental Rules

- **Ghost-State Traversal (Soul Form Mechanics):** When players fall in battle anywhere in the global ocean, their spirit wakes upon the shores of Xibalba. They may choose to complete an underworld trial or play a ballgame match to earn rapid resurrection with bonus spirit boons.
- **Shadow Light Dynamics:** Standard torches are snuffed out by underworld vapors. Only torches infused with obsidian resin or divine relics (such as *Xiuhcoatl*) provide illumination.
- **Respec Ritual (The Broth of Forgetfulness):** Drinking Meng Po's soup wipes a character's skill allocations, allowing complete reallocation of ability points.

---

## 4. Inhabitants & Factions

- **The Twelve Lords of Xibalba:** Hun-Came (One Death), Vucub-Came (Seven Death), and their plague-bearing lieutenants who preside over the trial houses.
- **Camazotz (The Bat of Death):** A colossal chiropteran monstrosity that drops from cave stalactites in the Bat House.
- **King Yanluo (Yama) & The Ox-Head and Horse-Face Guards:** The solemn judges and enforcers of Diyu who examine the karmic ledgers of fallen mortals.
- **Ah Puch (The Mayan God of Decay):** Roams the lower bone catacombs accompanied by an owl, offering dark necromantic trades.

---

## 5. Signature Relics & Local Loot

- **[Xiuhcoatl (The Turquoise Fire Serpent)](../tools-and-weapons/01-divine-weapons.md#6-xiuhcoatl)**: The living weapon of Huitzilopochtli, piercing underworld darkness with incandescent solar flame.
- **Meng Po's Jade Ladle:** A relic tool that brews potions of memory erasure and stat cleansing.
- **Obsidian Mirror of Tezcatlipoca:** Reveals hidden spirit paths, secret doors in cave walls, and hostile stealth entities.
