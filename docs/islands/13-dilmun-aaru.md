# Island Spec: Dilmun & The Field of Reeds (Aaru)

> *"The land of Dilmun is pure, the land of Dilmun is clean; the land of Dilmun is holy... In Dilmun the raven utters no cry, the lion kills not, the wolf snatches not the lamb. The sick-eyed says not 'I am sick-eyed'; the person with a headache says not 'I have a headache'."*  
> — *Enki and Ninhursag, Sumerian Mythological Tablet (c. 2000 BCE)*

---

## 1. Mythological Provenance

- **Traditions:** Sumerian / Mesopotamian (*Epic of Gilgamesh*, *Enki and Ninhursag*) & Ancient Egyptian (*Book of the Dead*, Papyrus of Ani, Pyramid Texts).
- **Historical Archetype:** The primordial garden of pure dawn (*Dilmun*), where Utnapishtim (the flood survivor) was placed by the gods to live forever, merged with *Aaru* (Sekhet-Aaru — The Field of Reeds), the Egyptian celestial afterlife governed by Osiris where barley grows seven cubits high and spirits harvest in eternal plenty.
- **Cosmological Foundation:** Located at the eastern convergence of the sweet-water abyss (*Abzu*) and the solar horizon (*Akhet*). It serves as the primary waystation for the Solar Barque of Ra (*Mesektet / Mandjet*) as it traverses between the day and night worlds.

### Key Sub-Zones

1. **The Pure Springs of Enki:** Crystal freshwater springs bubbling up from the earth where salt water cannot penetrate. Bathing in these pools cleanses all curses, poisons, and corruption statuses.
2. **The Seven-Cubit Barley Fields (Sekhet-Aaru):** Vast fields of radiant golden wheat and blue flax that can be harvested by players to craft high-tier provisions and offerings.
3. **The Hall of the Double Truth & The Scales of Ma'at:** A sandstone hypostyle hall with towering columns carved with hieroglyphic reliefs. In the center stands the colossal golden scale where Anubis tests the weight of hearts against the feather of truth.
4. **The Solar Barque Pier:** A sacred harbor crafted from polished white limestone and cedar of Lebanon, where the golden Sun Boat of Ra docks at sunset every game day.

---

## 3. Zonal Physics & Environmental Rules

- **The Divine Peace of Dilmun (Absolute Non-Aggression):** By primordial decree of Enki and Osiris, no mortal or god may unholster a lethal weapon on the island. Combat animations are disabled; weapons become sheathed ceremonial icons.
- **Eternal Rejuvenation:** Health and stamina bars are locked at 100% while within the borders of the central garden.
- **The Solar Transit Portal:** When Ra's Solar Barque docks, players carrying a *Solar Scarab* may board the vessel to embark on an oceanic express transit directly across the world to the western horizon.

---

## 4. Inhabitants & Factions

- **Utnapishtim the Faraway & His Wife:** Ancient Sumerian immortals who guard the secrets of the *Plant of Rejuvenation* (the prickly water-plant at the bottom of the deep).
- **The Ushabti Automatons:** Mummified clay and faience statuettes brought to life by hieroglyphic spells who tirelessly tend the irrigation canals and harvest the barley fields.
- **The Bennu Bird (The Egyptian Phoenix):** A magnificent golden-feathered heron crowned with the Atef crown, nesting atop the sacred Benben stone. Listening to its song grants a temporary resurrection boon.
- **Priests of Thoth (The Scribes of Eternity):** NPC scholars who translate undecipherable ancient tablets, decode starmaps, and record player world achievements.

---

## 5. Signature Relics & Local Loot

- **[The Was-Scepter & Sekhem](../tools-and-weapons/02-sacred-tools.md#6-the-sekhem--was-scepters)**: The Egyptian royal scepters of divine authority and primordial order (*Ma'at*), capable of pacifying rogue spirits and opening sealed crypt pylons.
- **The Plant of Rejuvenation (*Old Man Becomes Young*):** The prickly lotus-like marine succulent sought by Gilgamesh at the bottom of the island's freshwater abyss.
- **The Feather of Ma'at:** A weightless ostrich plume used as a focus for truth enchantments and judgment trials.

## 6. Implementation Notes for Three.js & Engine

- **Visual Palette:** Radiant warm Egyptian gold, lapis lazuli blues, pure alabaster white, and vivid papyrus greens. High-angle, brilliant golden directional lighting with soft sand-dune shadows.
- **Architecture:** Hypostyle halls with lotus-bud capitals, colossal seated granite statues, obelisks capped with electrum, and reflecting pools filled with blue lotus blossoms (*Nymphaea caerulea*).
- **Audio Ambience:** Sistrum rattles, ancient reed flutes (Ney), desert wind rustling through tall papyrus, and gentle fountain waters.
