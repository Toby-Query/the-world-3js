# Island Spec: Hawaiki & Onogoroshima

> *"Izanagi and Izanami stood upon the Floating Bridge of Heaven, and thrust down the Jeweled Spear of Heaven (Amenonuhoko) into the ocean mist, stirring the brine until it curdled with a curdling sound. When they drew up the spear, the brine that dripped from its tip piled up and became an island: Onogoroshima (the Self-Curdling Island)."*  
> — *Kojiki (Records of Ancient Matters, 712 CE)*

---

## 1. Mythological Provenance

- **Traditions:** Polynesian Oral Traditions (Māori, Hawaiian, Tahitian) & Japanese Shinto Mythology (*Kojiki*, *Nihon Shoki*).
- **Historical Archetype:** The dual genesis of oceanic land:
  1. **Hawaiki (Hawaiʻi / Pulotu):** The ancestral primordial island homeland of the Polynesian peoples from which the great voyagers embarked on double-hulled canoes (*Waka*), and where souls return after departure.
  2. **Onogoroshima (淤能碁呂島):** The first island of creation in Shinto mythology, formed when the heavenly jeweled spear *Amenonuhoko* churned the primordial ocean into curds of solid earth.
- **Cosmological Foundation:** The southern archipelago of living volcanoes, coral atoll rings, and geothermal currents. It represents oceanic genesis, mastery of the tides, and celestial wayfinding without artificial navigational aids.

### Key Sub-Zones

1. **The Spear Pillar of Onogoro:** A soaring spire of salt-encrusted obsidian stone at the island's center marking the spot where Izanagi's spear pierced the primordial sea. At its base stands a colossal vermilion wooden *Torii* gate rising directly from the breakers.
2. **The Kīlauea Magma Caldera (Sanctuary of Pele):** An active volcanic crater filled with bubbling molten basalt. Players can harness vertical thermal heat drafts to achieve sustained glider and flight ascents.
3. **The Ancestral Groves of Hawaiki:** Mist-shrouded rainforest valleys filled with towering koa trees, giant ferns, and sacred banyan trees where ancestral spirits (*Tūpuna*) grant blessings to respectful voyagers.
4. **The Bioluminescent Barrier Reef:** A living barrier reef glowing with cyan and emerald luminescence that protects the inner lagoon from open-sea monsters.

## 3. Zonal Physics & Environmental Rules

- **Celestial Wayfinding (No Mini-Map HUD):** When entering the waters around Hawaiki, the game's standard mini-map and compass HUD fade away. Players must navigate using realistic celestial constellations, wave swell patterns, bioluminescent trails, and bird flight paths.
- **Thermal Updraft Physics:** Volcanic steam vents and magma calderas provide powerful vertical velocity multipliers for gliders, wings, and flight abilities.
- **Tidal Shifting:** Low tide exposes land bridges between the surrounding atoll islands; high tide submerges them into treacherous coral mazes navigable only by outrigger canoes.

---

## 4. Inhabitants & Factions

- **The Polynesian Wayfinders (Tautai):** Master navigators who teach star paths, wind reading, canoe crafting, and oceanic tracking.
- **The Kami of Creation:** Shinto nature spirits inhabiting sacred rocks (*Iwakura*), ancient waterfalls, and venerable trees wrapped in *Shimenawa* straw ropes.
- **Menehune Crafters:** Legendary nocturnal dwarven artisans who build monumental stone masonry, fishponds, and aqueducts overnight.
- **Aumakua (Guardian Shark Spirits):** Colossal but benevolent hammerhead and reef sharks that escort friendly vessels through the reef passages while ramming hostile pirates.

## 5. Signature Relics & Local Loot

- **[Manaiakalani (The Fishhook of Māui)](../tools-and-weapons/02-sacred-tools.md#1-manaiakalani)**: The celestial bone fishhook capable of dredging sunken islands from the seafloor and hooking giant sea beasts.
- **[Amenonuhoko (The Heavenly Jeweled Spear)](../tools-and-weapons/02-sacred-tools.md#2-amenonuhoko)**: The creation spear that stabilizes shifting island terrain, purifies waters, and detects underwater mineral veins.
- **Lei Mano (Shark-Tooth War Club):** A hardwood weapon lined with fossilized Megalodon teeth that inflicts armor-cleaving lacerations.

## 6. Implementation Notes for Three.js & Engine

- **Terrain Shader:** Dynamic lava vertex displacement shader with glowing emissive cracks for volcanic sectors, transitioning into wet black volcanic sand and white coral beaches.
- **Vegetation:** Dense procedural tropical palm fronds, banana leaves, and crimson Ohia Lehua blossoms responding to wind velocity vectors.
- **Audio Ambience:** Rhythmic ocean surf crashing over coral barriers, ukulele and nose flute melodies, deep volcanic rumble, and seabird calls.
