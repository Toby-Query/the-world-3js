# Island Spec: The Necropolis of the 12 Gates & The Amduat

> *"Then Ra enters the First Hour of the Night, and the cavern doors swing open with a sound of thunder. The blessed spirits shout for joy, and the serpent who guards the gate bows before the solar glory... But in the twelfth hour, the great enemy Apep lies in wait beneath the sandbank to swallow the barque."*  
> — *The Book of the Amduat (Treatise of the Hidden Chamber, New Kingdom Egypt)*

---

## 1. Mythological Provenance

- **Traditions:** Ancient Egyptian Mortuary Texts (*The Book of the Dead*, *The Book of Gates*, *The Amduat*).
- **Thematic Core:** The nocturnal journey of the sun god Ra aboard the Solar Barque (*Mesektet*) through the subterranean underworld (**Duat**), passing through twelve guarded gates representing the twelve hours of night; the weighing of the heart against the Feather of Truth (*Ma'at*) in the hall of Osiris, and the cosmic nightly battle against the chaos serpent **Apep (Apophis)**.
- **Adaptation into an Island:** In *The World*, this island appears as an imposing desert necropolis rising from the black night-waters of the southern ocean. Ringed by monumental sandstone pylons and avenues of sphinxes, its interior descends into a series of twelve monumental hypostyle caverns, each representing an hour of the night.

### Key Sub-Zones

1. **The Avenue of Ram-Headed Sphinxes:** A grand paved ceremonial avenue leading from the coastal limestone quays to the first pylon gate, flanked by hundreds of granite sphinxes holding golden ankhs.
2. **The Hall of Judgment (The Scales of Ma'at):** An immense subterranean chamber supported by lotus-capital columns. In the center stands the towering bronze and gold scales where Anubis tests the weight of players' souls against an ostrich feather.
3. **The Lake of Fire:** A glowing pool of liquid sulfur and divine flame where the souls of the unrighteous are purified, guarded by four fiery baboons.
4. **The Sandbank of Apep (The 12th Hour):** A pitch-black cavern where the giant serpent of chaos coils beneath the dunes. Every in-game night, a server-wide raid event spawns here to defend the Solar Barque from being swallowed before dawn.

## 3. Zonal Physics & Environmental Rules

- **The Light of Ra (Aura of Solar Ward):** Unprotected players wandering the deep caverns suffer *Darkness Decay* (stamina drain and blind hallucinations). Standing near the Solar Barque or carrying a solar scarab projects a radiant protective aura.
- **The Karmic Weight Check:** Players who have engaged in excessive unprovoked player-killing (high infamy) will find their heart outweighs the feather of Ma'at at the scales, triggering an encounter with the beast **Ammit**. Players with clean records receive divine blessings from Osiris.
- **Eternal Embalming (Mummification Buff):** Consuming natron salts and wrapping wounds in linen bandages doubles healing efficiency and grants temporary poison immunity.

---

## 4. Inhabitants & Factions

- **Anubis (The Opener of the Ways):** The jackal-headed god who oversees embalming, guides souls through the gates, and adjudicates the scale of truth.
- **The Ushabti Guardians:** Mummified stone sentinels armed with golden khopesh sickles who awaken to repel grave robbers and trespassers.
- **Apep (The Chaos Serpent):** A monstrous leviathan snake of infinite length that tries to swallow the sun, vulnerable only to enchanted obsidian spears and solar magic.
- **Scribes of Thoth:** Ibis-headed scholars who transcribe player histories and teach ancient hieroglyphic inscriptions.

---

## 5. Signature Relics & Local Loot

- **The Khopesh of Horus:** A curved bronze sickle-sword that emits radiant solar slashes capable of cleaving shadow beasts and dispelling darkness.
- **The Heart Scarab of Purity:** An amulet carved from green jasper that places a thumb on the scales of judgment, preventing negative consequences during the Weighing of the Heart.
- **The Eye of Ra (Udjat):** An amulet that fires a devastating ray of concentrated solar wrath, vaporizing incoming dark projectiles.

## 6. Implementation Notes for Three.js & Engine

- **Visual Palette:** Glowing desert sandstone, rich electrum gold, lapis lazuli, turquoise, and deep obsidian shadows.
- **Lighting:** Torches and braziers cast warm flickering orange light with sharp geometric shadows on carved hieroglyphic walls.
- **Audio:** Sistrum chimes, low resonant horn blasts, the hiss of subterranean serpents, and deep ceremonial chanting.
