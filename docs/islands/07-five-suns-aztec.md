# Island Spec: The City of the Five Suns & The Obsidian Altars

> *"It was told that there were four suns before this one. The first perished by jaguars; the second by hurricane; the third by a rain of fire; the fourth by deluge... And now we dwell in the Fifth Sun, the Sun of Movement (Nahui-Ollin), which is destined to perish by earthquakes and the descent of the Star Demons from the heavens."*  
> — *Legend of the Suns (Leyenda de los Soles, Codex Chimalpopoca)*

---

## 1. Mythological Provenance

- **Traditions:** Aztec / Nahua / Mexica Mythology (*Codex Borgia*, *Florentine Codex*, *Leyenda de los Soles*).
- **Thematic Core:** The cyclical cosmic creation and destruction of the **Five Suns** (Nahui-Ocelotl, Nahui-Ehecatl, Nahui-Quiahuitl, Nahui-Atl, and the current Nahui-Ollin); the descent of the feathered serpent god **Quetzalcoatl** into the underworld of Mictlan to gather the bones of past generations; the warrior sun god **Huitzilopochtli**, and the terror of the celestial star demons (**Tzitzimime**) who descend during solar eclipses.
- **Adaptation into an Island:** In *The World*, this island is a massive stepped volcanic plateau surrounded by a shimmering turquoise lagoon. At its center stands a colossal stepped pyramid (*Teocalli*) crowned by the monolithic Sun Stone calendar. Five distinct districts on the island correspond to the five elemental cosmic ages: the Obsidian Jaguar Jungle, the Wind Canyon, the Rain of Cinders, the Water Cenote, and the Golden Earthquake Temple.

### Key Sub-Zones

1. **The Great Teocalli Pyramid:** A towering stepped pyramid with 365 steps. At the summit, the massive circular Sun Stone calendar rotates, tracking cosmic alignment and forecasting server-wide eclipse events.
2. **The Obsidian Jaguar Jungle (Nahui-Ocelotl):** A dense jungle where trees sprout jagged black volcanic glass leaves. Shadow jaguars stalk the underbrush, phasing in and out of the ethereal plane.
3. **The Wind Canyon of Quetzalcoatl (Nahui-Ehecatl):** A roaring chasm carved by perpetual hurricane gales. Wind-temple flutes echo through the rocks, and players can ride wind currents using feathered serpent cloaks.
4. **The Chinampas (Floating Island Gardens):** A network of reed and soil rafts anchored to the shallow lagoon bed by willow trees, producing rare medicinal maize, cacao, and sacred amaranth crops.

## 3. Zonal Physics & Environmental Rules

- **The Eclipse of the Fifth Sun:** Periodically, the moon eclipses the sun over the island, plunging the world into total twilight. During the eclipse, the monstrous skeletal star demons (*Tzitzimime*) descend from the skybox, attacking anything outside sanctuary wards until the eclipse ends.
- **Obsidian Cleaving (Razor Fracture):** Weapons forged from the island's green and black volcanic glass (*Itztli*) inflict compounding bleed lacerations that pierce heavy metal armor, but have lower durability under sustained blunt impacts.
- **The Solar Offering Meter:** Players can donate solar gems and energy to the altar of Huitzilopochtli to prolong the daylight cycle and forestall the next eclipse raid.

---

## 4. Inhabitants & Factions

- **Eagle and Jaguar Knights (Cuāuhtli & Ocēlōtl):** Elite warrior brotherhoods clad in feathered regalia and spotted jaguar pelts, wielding obsidian *macuahuitl* clubs and atlatl dart-throwers.
- **Quetzalcoatl (The Feathered Serpent):** An emerald-and-gold feathered dragon deity who circles the upper sky, teaching astronomy, metallurgy, and peaceful agriculture.
- **The Tzitzimime (Star Demons):** Terrifying skeletal celestial horrors with razor claws and star-cluster eyes who descend from the heavens during darkness.
- **Priests of the Smoking Mirror (Tezcatlipoca):** Sorcerers who use polished obsidian mirrors to gaze across server distances, track enemy movements, and cast curses of discord.

---

## 5. Signature Relics & Local Loot

- **The Macuahuitl of Tonatiuh:** A heavy oak war club lined with razor-sharp prismatic obsidian blades that glow with solar embers on high combo hits.
- **Feathered Cloak of Quetzalcoatl:** Grants the ability to glide gracefully through wind corridors and speak the universal tongue with all beast-kin.
- **Obsidian Mirror of Tezcatlipoca:** Reveals the true location of cloaked players, hidden portals, and impending world events.

## 6. Implementation Notes for Three.js & Engine

- **Architecture:** Stepped pyramids with serpent-head balustrades, stucco-relief friezes in brilliant red, turquoise, and yellow ochre.
- **Visual FX:** Shimmering obsidian reflections using high-gloss roughness maps, dynamic solar corona effects during eclipse sequences.
- **Audio:** Deep huehuetl wooden drums, teponaztli slit drums, clay death whistles (*Ehecacozcatl*) that produce terrifying wind screams, and jungle bird calls.
