# Island Spec: The Taiga of the Dancing Hut & Koschei's Needle

> *"Turn your back to the forest, and your front to me! And the hut turned on its giant fowl legs with a great creaking and stood still... Around the hut ran a palisade of bleached bones, and upon each stake was mounted a skull whose hollow eye-sockets blazed with cold white fire."*  
> — *Russian Fairy Tales (Narodnye Russkie Skazki, Aleksandr Afanasyev)*

---

## 1. Mythological Provenance

- **Traditions:** Slavic Folklore & East European Fairy Tales (*Skazki*, Byliny).
- **Thematic Core:** The enigmatic wilderness witch **Baba Yaga**; her mobile hut that walks upon giant chicken legs; her flying mortar and pestle with which she sweeps away her tracks; the sorcerer **Koschei the Deathless** whose soul is sealed inside a needle, inside an egg, inside a duck, inside a hare, inside an iron chest buried beneath a sacred green oak; and the radiant **Firebird** whose feathers burn like living coals.
- **Adaptation into an Island:** In *The World*, this island appears as an endless, ancient taiga of silver birches, towering black firs, and mossy bogs wrapped in perpetual twilight. The island is famous for containing the **Walking Dungeon**: Baba Yaga's chicken-legged hut constantly strides through the forest, requiring players to track and confront it dynamically.

### Key Sub-Zones

1. **The Dancing Hut (Izbushka na Kuryikh Nozhkakh):** A rustic log cabin standing atop two massive bird legs that strides between tree canopies. It rotates to face players only when commanded with the traditional invocation. Inside lies Baba Yaga's hearth, iron cauldrons, and rare potion recipes.
2. **The Sacred Oak of Koschei:** A mountain-scale oak tree whose roots entangle the iron chest of Koschei the Deathless. Defeating Koschei requires a multi-stage puzzle: opening the chest, chasing the hare, shooting down the duck, retrieving the egg, and snapping the needle within.
3. **The Firebird's Clearing:** A glowing clearing in the deep forest where the *Zhar-ptitsa* perches in golden apple trees. Gathering its fallen feathers provides light in the deepest darkness without consuming torch fuel.
4. **The Twin Wells of Life and Death:** Two bubbling forest springs side-by-side:
   - *Myortvaya Voda (Dead Water):* When poured over a severed limb or destroyed object, knits the broken pieces seamlessly back together.
   - *Zhivaya Voda (Living Water):* Restores breath and soul to the knitted vessel.

## 3. Zonal Physics & Environmental Rules

- **The Living Forest (Path-Shifting Trees):** The trees of the taiga subtly shift positions when players turn their cameras away, disorienting standard navigation and making straight-line travel impossible without following animal tracks or using Firebird feathers.
- **Mobile Dungeon Physics:** The Dancing Hut's interior exists in a stable reference frame even while the hut walks, stomps, and leaps across chasms in the exterior world.
- **The Mortar-and-Pestle Aerial Flight:** Players who earn Baba Yaga's favor can pilot a wooden mortar and pestle through the air, steering with a birch broom that erases their radar signature from enemy maps.

---

## 4. Inhabitants & Factions

- **Baba Yaga:** The ancient bone-legged forest witch. She can be a deadly boss or a crafty benefactor depending on whether players observe proper etiquette and bring her sweet pastries and iron thimbles.
- **Koschei the Deathless:** A skeletal, immortal sorcerer-king impervious to all standard attacks until his hidden needle is snapped.
- **The Leshy (Forest Lord):** A shape-shifting spirit who grows as tall as a tree in the forest and as short as a blade of grass in the meadow, leading lost players in circles unless they put their boots on the wrong feet.
- **Rusalki (Water Maidens):** Pale, green-haired sirens inhabiting the peat bogs who try to tickle swimming players to death unless offered ribbons or comb gifts.

---

## 5. Signature Relics & Local Loot

- **The Firebird's Golden Feather:** Radiates warm golden light that dispels freezing debuffs and exposes hidden spirits within a 30-meter radius.
- **The Self-Spreading Tablecloth (Skrat-Samobranka):** An embroidered linen cloth that, when laid upon the ground, immediately conjures a bountiful feast for party members.
- **Koschei's Iron Needle:** The snapped phylactery needle, useful as a crafting core for items that grant second-chance resurrection charges.
- **Vial of Living & Dead Waters:** Essential reagents for repairing shattered legendary weapons and resurrecting party companions without stat penalties.

## 6. Implementation Notes for Three.js & Engine

- **Visual FX:** Deep autumnal and winter palettes with golden-yellow birch leaves, dark evergreen pines, and soft white snow cover. Glowing cold-white light emanating from skull eyes on the palisade.
- **Animation:** Rigged procedural walk cycle for the two giant avian legs supporting Baba Yaga's wooden cabin.
- **Audio:** Howling autumn wind through bare birch branches, the creak of wooden logs, eerie balalaika melodies, and the heavy thudding footsteps of the hut.
