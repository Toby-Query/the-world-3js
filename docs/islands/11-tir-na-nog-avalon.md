# Island Spec: Tír na nÓg & Avalon (Ynys Afallon)

> *"There is a distant isle, around which sea-horses glisten... A delightful land through all the ages, across which blossoms drop. Death, cold, or the decaying of sickness comes not to anyone."*  
> — *The Voyage of Bran (Immram Brain), 8th-century Old Irish*

---

## 1. Mythological Provenance

- **Traditions:** Celtic Mythology (Irish *Otherworld / Tír na nÓg*), Arthurian Legend (*Avalon / Ynys Afallon*), Welsh *Mabinogion*.
- **Historical Archetype:** The western island of eternal youth, golden apples of longevity, sovereign healing, and the sovereign burial barrow of King Arthur watched by Morgan le Fay.
- **Cosmological Foundation:** In Gaelic mythology, the Otherworld (*Aos Sí*) is accessed across the western sea (*Mag Mell* — The Plain of Delight). Time operates on a catastrophic dilation: mortal wanderer Oisín spent what felt like three years in Tír na nÓg with the fairy princess Niamh, only to discover three centuries had passed in the mortal realm upon touching mortal soil.

### Key Sub-Zones

1. **The Féth Fíada (The Silver Mist Curtain):** A dense magical fog ringing the island. Ships without an enchanted branch or navigational guide sail in circles and emerge back into the open sea without ever seeing the shore.
2. **The Silver Apple Orchards of Avalon:** Perpetual blossom trees whose fruit radiates a soft golden bioluminescence. Eating the fruit halts aging counters and cures any chronic condition.
3. **The Well of Segais:** A sacred freshwater spring surrounded by nine sacred hazel trees. Hazelnuts of wisdom drop into the water, producing bubbles of pure inspiration; drinking from the spring grants spellcasting mastery and unlocks ancient lore.
4. **The Barrow of the King:** A monolithic stone mound crowned by a circle of ancient standing stones (menhirs) where the wounded sovereign sleeps, watched over by nine queens.

---

## 3. Zonal Physics & Environmental Rules

- **Temporal Dilation:** Time inside the realm flows asynchronously: 1 hour spent exploring the interior is equivalent to 24 hours of elapsed time on the mortal ocean. Players who step outside without grounding wards temporarily suffer rapid stamina fatigue.
- **The Sanctuary Law of Danu:** No player or NPC can be permanently killed within the borders of the island. Fatal damage puts players into a restorative coma (*Slumber of Avalon*) where they are tended by dryads.
- **The Panacea Aura:** The island is the **sole global cure location** for specific realm-wide plagues (such as the *Blight of Niflheim* or the *Red Tide of Sekhmet*).

---

## 4. Inhabitants & Factions

- **The Tuatha Dé Danann (Aos Sí):** Ethereal fae nobles, harpists, and enchanters who speak in riddle and poetry.
- **The Nine Sisters of Avalon:** Powerful enchantresses led by Morgan le Fay who brew potions, forge mystic scabbards, and tend to wounded sovereigns.
- **Púca & Kelpie Beasts:** Shape-shifting tricksters roaming the outer shorelines. A kelpie will offer a ride across the water, only to attempt dragging unsuspecting players into the surf.

---

## 5. Signature Relics & Local Loot

- **[Cauldron of the Dagda (The Undry)](../tools-and-weapons/02-sacred-tools.md#5-the-cauldron-of-the-dagda)**: A bottomless bronze vessel from Murias that produces unlimited provisions and brews cure-alls.
- **[Fragarach (The Answerer)](../tools-and-weapons/01-divine-weapons.md#4-gae-bulg--fragarach)**: The sword of Manannán mac Lir and Lugh; no armor can withstand its blade, and anyone with its point at their throat cannot tell a lie.
- **Silver Bough with Golden Apples:** A musical branch from Manannán that lulls hostile entities to sleep when shaken.

## 6. Implementation Notes for Three.js & Engine

- **Visual Tone:** Soft, dream-like color grading with rich emerald grasses, blooming lavender heather, and shimmering volumetric fog planes using Three.js custom shaders.
- **Water Shaders:** Clear emerald-green freshwater lagoons with caustic reflections and floating pink water-lilies.
- **Atmospheric Particle System:** Drifting silver dandelion seeds, floating golden pollen, and gossamer fairy dust motes.
