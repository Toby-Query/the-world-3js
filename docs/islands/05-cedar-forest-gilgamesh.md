# Island Spec: The Sacred Cedar Forest & The Gates of Uruk

> *"Humbaba's roar is a deluge, his mouth is fire, his breath is death! He can hear the rustle of the cedar tree sixty leagues away. Why would you enter the forest where the god Enlil has stationed him to keep the cedars safe?"*  
> — *The Epic of Gilgamesh (Tablet II, c. 2100 BCE)*

---

## 1. Mythological Provenance

- **Traditions:** Ancient Mesopotamian / Sumerian & Babylonian Epics (*The Epic of Gilgamesh*, *Epic of Atrahasis*).
- **Thematic Core:** The journey of King Gilgamesh and his wild companion Enkidu to achieve eternal fame by penetrating the forbidden mountain-sized **Cedar Forest**, confronting its guardian **Humbaba the Terrible**, defying the divine Bull of Heaven sent by Ishtar, and seeking the secret of immortality across the waters of death.
- **Adaptation into an Island:** In the endless ocean, this island rises as a mountainous cedar redoubt wrapped in primeval fog. The coastal lowlands feature the massive baked-brick ramparts of Uruk inlaid with blue lapis lazuli tiles, while the interior is dominated by trees so ancient and titanic that their canopies block out the sun entirely.

### Key Sub-Zones

1. **The Baked-Brick Ramparts of Uruk:** The coastal city-state founded by the Seven Sages, crowned with blue glazed-tile friezes of lions and palm trees. Players can trade cedar timber for bronze and lapis lazuli goods.
2. **The Impact Crater of the Bull of Heaven:** A smoking, glass-bottomed crater where the celestial bull struck the earth, fracturing the ground into three hundred fissures that vent subterranean steam.
3. **The Primeval Cedar Forest:** A dense alpine forest of colossal *Cedrus libani* whose trunks measure 20 meters in diameter. Harvesting this sacred wood yields the strongest shipbuilding material in the game, but doing so increases Humbaba's wrath meter across the island.
4. **The Lair of Humbaba (The Roaring Valley):** A wind-scoured mountain gorge where the guardian roams, draped in seven cloaks of terrifying magical radiance (*Melammu*).

## 3. Zonal Physics & Environmental Rules

- **Acoustic Shockwave Deluge:** Humbaba's periodic territorial roars trigger acoustic shockwaves that can be felt across the entire island, causing falling cedar branches, rockslides, and temporarily deafening players who are not sheltered in caves or behind thick walls.
- **Titan Wood Density:** Timber harvested from the sacred cedars is immune to saltwater rot and possesses triple buoyancy, making ships built with it 50% faster and near-unsinkable.
- **The Seven Cloaks of Terror (Melammu Aura):** Approaching Humbaba's lair without an offering of friendship or divine wind wards causes players' screens to blur with psychological dread and stamina paralysis.

---

## 4. Inhabitants & Factions

- **Humbaba the Terrible (Island Titan):** The ancient guardian appointed by Enlil. Wields a colossal mace of petrified cedar; his breath causes scorching damage and his roar can blow back player ships anchored offshore.
- **Enkidu's Wild Kin:** Horned humanoids and wild gazelle riders who patrol the outer forest borders, living in harmony with nature and teaching players beast-taming arts.
- **Scribes of the Seven Sages (Apkallu):** Robed fish-cloaked sages who teach cuneiform inscription, enabling players to record contracts and laws on clay tablets that cannot be breached by player betrayals.
- **Scorpion-Men of Mount Mashu:** Half-human, half-scorpion guardians stationed at the entrance to the sun's subterranean transit tunnels.

---

## 5. Signature Relics & Local Loot

- **The Axe of Gilgamesh (Three Talents of Bronze):** A massive two-handed battleaxe that can fell titan trees and cleave monster carapaces with heavy kinetic impact.
- **The Prickly Plant of Rejuvenation (*Old Man Becomes Young*):** Found growing in the freshwater sea-springs off the island's southern reef; consuming it resets character physical age counters and restores all stamina instantly.
- **Lapis Lazuli Cuneiform Tablet of Destinies (Fragment):** Grants the bearer foresight into the exact spawn times of global world events.

---

## 6. Implementation Notes for Three.js & Engine

- **Vegetation:** Massive custom-modeled cedar trees with volumetric sunbeams filtering through dense needles using Three.js `SpotLight` shadow maps.
- **Textures:** Baked mud-brick textures with lapis lazuli glazed brick normal maps for Uruk's ramparts.
- **Audio:** Deep guttural beast roars echoing through mountain valleys, the thud of heavy axes biting into dense cedar wood, and wind whistling through pine needles.
