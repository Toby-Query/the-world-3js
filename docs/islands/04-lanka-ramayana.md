# Island Spec: The Golden Citadel of Lanka & The Monkey Bridge

> *"Then the mighty Vanaras, under the command of Nala and Nila, cast trees and colossal rocks into the ocean. The stones inscribed with the holy name did not sink to the ocean floor, but floated upon the waters... Thus was constructed the wondrous bridge across the salt sea, ten leagues wide and a hundred leagues long, stretching straight to the golden gates of Lanka."*  
> — *Valmiki Ramayana (Yuddha Kanda)*

---

## 1. Mythological Provenance

- **Traditions:** Ancient Indian Epic (*The Ramayana* by Sage Valmiki).
- **Thematic Core:** The climactic battle between Prince Rama and the ten-headed rakshasa king Ravana; the architectural wonder of the floating sea-causeway (**Rama Setu / Setubandha**) built by the Vanara army, and the impenetrable golden citadel of **Lanka**, designed by the celestial architect Vishwakarma.
- **Adaptation into an Island:** In *The World*, Lanka rises from tropical turquoise waters as an impossible mountain of pure golden spires and tiered Dravidian gopurams. Extending from its western shoals across miles of open sea is the floating stone causeway, where massive carved stones float upon the water's surface as if weightless.

### Key Sub-Zones

1. **The Floating Causeway (Rama Setu):** A sprawling marine highway constructed of colossal carved boulders floating directly atop the ocean swells. Players can cooperate to extend, repair, and defend this bridge from sea raiders.
2. **The Golden Citadel of Lanka:** A vertical megacity forged of celestial gold (*Suvarna*) that catches the sun in blinding reflections. The city features nine concentric walls protected by mechanical iron arrows and flaming cauldrons.
3. **The Ashoka Vatika:** A serene, fragrance-drenched sacred grove of flowering scarlet Ashoka trees and wild peacocks, protected from the war outside by ancient ascetic mantras.
4. **The Hangar of the Pushpaka Vimana:** A mountaintop platform housing the mythical aerial chariot *Pushpaka Vimana*, an ancient flying vessel that moves at the speed of thought.

---

## 3. Zonal Physics & Environmental Rules

- **Buoyancy Inversion (The Floating Stone Rule):** Certain heavy volcanic and limestone boulders found around the island do not sink; when dropped into water, they lock into place as floating platforms. Players can throw them into the sea to create temporary stepping stones across boiling waters.
- **Golden Solar Amplification:** Solar-aligned weapons and celestial abilities deal +35% damage while within Lanka due to sunlight reverberating off the gold architecture.
- **The Ten-Directional Ward:** In the upper palace, directional wind blasts blow players off narrow golden walkways unless they anchor themselves or use heavy boots.

---

## 4. Inhabitants & Factions

- **The Vanara Warriors:** Mythical simian warriors endowed with immense strength, agility, and devotion. They teach boulder-tossing, tree-top sprinting, and the crafting of floating stone causeways.
- **The Rakshasa Guard:** Towering multi-armed illusionist warriors who can grow to the size of hills, alter their shapes, and wield burning clubs.
- **The Ascetic Hanumān (World Guardian):** The legendary son of the wind god Vayu. Occasionally leaps across the sky from island to island, leaving a sonic shockwave trail that clears stormy weather across the sea.
- **Vibhishana the Righteous:** Ravana's brother who defected to dharma, acting as a diplomatic mentor and vendor of rare celestial astras.

---

## 5. Signature Relics & Local Loot

- **Inscribed Floating Boulder of Nala:** A throwable building block that floats permanently on water, allowing players to build bridges and sea forts anywhere in the global ocean.
- **Feather of the Pushpaka Vimana:** Grants temporary levitation and directional aerial maneuvering.
- **Brahmashira Astra (Fragment):** A terrifying celestial arrow tip that burns with the heat of a thousand suns, dealing catastrophic area-of-effect damage to fortress gates.

## 6. Implementation Notes for Three.js & Engine

- **Visual Palette:** Dazzling metallic gold, vibrant saffron orange, rich emerald jungle foliage, and deep lapis lazuli ocean water.
- **Shaders:** PBR gold material with high specular reflectivity and complex Dravidian stone relief normal maps.
- **Audio:** Resonant mridangam drums, blowing of conch shells (*Shankha*), chanting of Sanskrit victory mantras, and the calls of tropical peacocks.
