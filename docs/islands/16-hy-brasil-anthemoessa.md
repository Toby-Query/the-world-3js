# Island Spec: Hy-Brasil & Anthemoessa (The Shrouded Isle & Siren Shoals)

> *"On the sea west of Ireland lies an enchanted island, circular in form, divided by a curved river... cloaked in perpetual fog and appearing only once every seven years to mortal eyes."*  
> — *Medieval Irish Sea Lore & The Chart of Angelino Dulcert (1339)*

---

## 1. Mythological Provenance

- **Traditions:** Irish Maritime Folklore (*Hy-Brasil / High Brazil / O'Breasil*) & Classical Greek Epic (*Homer's Odyssey*, *Apollonius of Rhodes' Argonautica*).
- **Historical Archetype:** The phase-shifting phantom island and the seductive maritime hazard:
  1. **Hy-Brasil:** The mythical Atlantic phantom island mapped on maritime charts from 1325 to the 19th century, shrouded in thick mist, home to giant black rabbits and an enigmatic ancient civilization that mastered temporal mechanics.
  2. **Anthemoessa (Ἀνθεμόεσσα):** The flower-filled meadow island where the Sirens (Parthenope, Ligeia, Leucosia) sang enchantments to passing sailors, surrounded by shores piled high with the rotting bones and bleached hides of wrecked voyagers.
- **Cosmological Foundation:** Located at the turbulent boundary between the Western Mystical Current and the Open Ocean. It serves as a navigational puzzle island and high-risk lure zone.

### Key Sub-Zones

1. **The Circular Moat & Island of Brasil:** A perfectly circular island divided neatly through the center by a crescent-shaped river. At its apex stands a circular stone citadel whose architecture matches no known historical era, surrounded by ancient stone automatons.
2. **The Flower Meadow of Anthemoessa:** A deceptive paradise of sweet-smelling scarlet poppies and white asphodel blossoms, contrasting sharply with the heaps of human bones concealed beneath the tall grass.
3. **The Siren Crags:** Jagged limestone sea stacks ringing the eastern approach where winged Sirens perch, projecting auditory illusions and songs that charm ship rudders toward the razor reefs.
4. **The Graveyard of Ships:** A labyrinth of shattered galleys, triremes, and modern steel cargo ships impaled on submerged needle rocks.

## 3. Zonal Physics & Environmental Rules

- **Phase-Shifting Temporal Cycle:** Hy-Brasil is only fully material and accessible during specific calendar alignments (e.g. during full moon phases or server world events). At other times, it appears as an insubstantial phantom silhouette that ships pass straight through.
- **Auditory Charm Effect (The Siren Song):** Players steering ships into Anthemoessa's waters hear hypnotic singing that inverts ship steering controls and forces characters to walk toward the bow unless their ears are stopped with beeswax (*Odyssean Wax*) or counter-sung by a bard wielding Orpheus' lyre.
- **Dense Radar/Compass Scrambler:** Compass needles spin erratically, and mini-map bearings drift by up to 90 degrees due to high magnetic lodestone deposits.

---

## 4. Inhabitants & Factions

- **The Sirens (Children of Achelous):** Avian-humanoid enchantresses with dazzling plumage and lethal talons who lure travelers into jagged shoals.
- **The Keepers of Brasil:** Ancient robed astronomers and giant nocturnal black hares who maintain the island's chronometer towers and trade rare temporal relics.
- **Spectral Argonauts:** Ghostly sailors doomed to row forever in the mist until released by breaking the Siren Queen's song-stone.

---

## 5. Signature Relics & Local Loot

- **[Oshun's Brass Mirror](../tools-and-weapons/03-relics-and-regalia.md#5-oshuns-brass-mirror--fan)**: The divine mirror of the Yoruba goddess of sweet waters, which dispels sonic charms, reveals illusions, and deflects petrifying gaze attacks.
- **Odyssean Beeswax Plugs:** Consumable item crafted from Mount Olympus honeycomb that grants 15 minutes of complete immunity to charm and sonic disorientation.
- **Golden Lyre of Orpheus:** An instrument capable of overriding the siren song with harmonious melodies that calm turbulent sea waves.

## 6. Implementation Notes for Three.js & Engine

- **Visual FX:** Animated rolling sea fog with localized density falloffs using custom Three.js shader uniforms. Ghostly chromatic aberration effects when approaching the island's phase boundaries.
- **Audio System:** Dynamic positional 3D audio for siren melodies that pan across headphones to disorient player navigation.
- **Lighting:** Eerie twilight gloom pierced by intermittent lighthouse beams and bioluminescent asphodel blossoms.
